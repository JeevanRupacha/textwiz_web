Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which the TextWiz mobile application (the "App") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Information Collected and Stored

User-Generated Content (Widget Text)

The primary data processed by the App is the text and customization settings you input for your home screen widget.

  • Storage Location: All user-entered text and preferences are stored **locally** on your device.
  • Data Transmission: This user-generated content is **never** transmitted to us, or to any third party, over the internet. We do not have access to, or maintain copies of, the text you create.

No Personal Identification Information (PII) Collected

We do not require you to provide us with any personal identification information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number to use the App.

3. Log Data and Tracking

The App does not use any third-party services for analytics, advertising, or tracking. The App does not collect or log any data about your device, your location, or your usage of the App.

If the App were to encounter an error, standard system logs may be generated by your operating system, but we do not access, collect, or review these logs.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. Given that we do not collect any personal data, we cannot knowingly collect information from children. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete this from any stored records (though, as stated, no such records are maintained by us).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
